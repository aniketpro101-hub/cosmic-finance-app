## ⚖️ 5. PERFORMANCE, USABILITY & COMPLIANCE GUARDRAILS

To ensure Cosmic Finance remains lightning fast, perfectly accessible, and fully compliant:

1. **60 FPS Mobile Performance Rule**:
   - Heavy animations must be restricted to GPU-accelerated CSS properties (`transform` and `opacity`).
   - Mobile viewports must automatically deactivate mouse-following cursors and intensive 3D particle math, falling back to lightweight, battery-friendly CSS animations.
2. **Accessibility & Reduced Motion**:
   - Every animation must respect the user's `prefers-reduced-motion` operating system setting, providing instantaneous, clean fades instead of movement for sensitive users.
3. **SEBI & Educational Harmony**:
   - Animations must never mimic gambling wheels, casino slot machines, or "get-rich-quick" excitement. Every motion must convey contemplation, mathematical precision, historical wisdom, and serious long-term risk discipline.
